# Label review: the 12 ambiguous items

Reviewed by Henry on 25 October 2026: all 12 labels accepted as proposed. The set is frozen; see `eval/FROZEN.md`.

Time limits: 2 days for a serious public health threat (MDR Art. 87(4)), 10 days for death or an unanticipated serious deterioration (Art. 87(5)), 15 days otherwise (Art. 87(3)). "Unanticipated" is judged only against the device's anticipated harms in `eval/devices.json`.

Patient is fine. | Possible serious incident, 15 days | No harm with saline, but the same over-delivery with a high-alert drug might lead to serious deterioration (criterion B: if it happened again). Conservative under Art. 87(7). 15 days. | Agree |
